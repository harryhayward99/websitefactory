Questions, answered.

What happens at the first appointment?

Your initial consultation is unhurried. Sophie listens to your history, assesses how you move, and explains a clear plan before treatment continues.

What can Sophie help with?

Initial consultation, continued treatment, sports massage, cupping where suitable, and an introductory offer for new clients.

How do I book?

Use the Book buttons, call 01926 854 210, or send an enquiry through the contact form. Sophie usually confirms appointments the same day.

Where is the clinic?

The clinic is at 14 Abbey End, Kenilworth, CV8 1QJ, with nearby parking on Abbey End and around the town centre.

What are the opening times?

Monday to Thursday 09:00–18:00, Friday 09:00–17:00 and Saturday 09:00–13:00. Sunday is closed.
