Why Us

  • Excellent home/School communication channels. Reports can be supplied to schools by arrangement and attendance at school meetings can also be arranged.

Why Schools Choose Us

  • Tutors will liaise closely with the class teacher to work on initial targets.
  • We offer a free consultation with teachers to discuss the needs of the learner(s) both behavioural and academic.
  • All planning is provided and detailed records are kept.
  • Recommendations for further work in the classroom is given.
  • Schools can choose from an informal assessment only or assessment and teaching for dyslexic students or those exhibiting literacy difficulties.

Specialist Dyslexia Tuition

Children may just need more time to assimilate information or need to be offered alternative ways of learning and the time to ask questions that a busy classroom can’t always accommodate.

We have discovered that giving children this extra time to talk to a tutor, work at their own pace and ask questions as they build a relationship with their tutor, allows their confidence and self-belief to grow steadily.

We provide informal assessments to determine the intervention needed for literacy difficulties and suspected dyslexia. If your child has a formal diagnosis, we use that report to guide our programme. The assessment results shape a personalised weekly plan, featuring multi-sensory overlearning and varied tasks to maintain focus and embed learning.
