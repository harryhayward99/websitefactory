Meet the practitioner

Deborah Coleman

Podiatrist · BSc (Hons) MChS · HCPC CH8559

Deborah opened Tidy Feet in Southam in September 2007 and has practised in Stratford-upon-Avon since 2010. A former nurse (RGN), she holds a BSc (Hons) MChS, is a member of the Royal College of Podiatrists, and is registered with the HCPC (CH8559). She has been qualified since 2007.

Patient reviews

Tidy Feet client

Mr N

I am 71 years, and still follow my profession as a shepherd. Since I walk a great deal, and have done so all my life, my feet are very important to me. The last few years I have had problems with a severe fungal growth under the nail of my right big toe. This toe is also somewhat distorted due to having been trodden on many times over the years by horses. Mrs Deborah Coleman has treated and trimmed the nail of this toe, and the rest of my toes, and this has my feet much more comfortable, and my right big toe much less painful. I am very grateful for this treatment.

Tidy Feet client

Mrs G

I am a 66 year old female who visited Tidy Feet after a fall, and post concussion syndrome. I have been delighted with the results of my two visits with Deborah Coleman at Tidy Feet. Her interest in foot health encompasses a whole body approach, and I could, listening to her, see that my heel pain had a larger context in which the symptoms were exacerbated. Her diagnosis was spot on. She demonstrated what she was needing to do, and why, and how to maintain the effect of immediate relief. Could not have had a more effective treatment. Her recommendation as to shoes that would work, plus posture and exercise have continued to keep me well.

Tidy Feet client

Mrs M

When my daughter, aged 5, developed an ingrowing toenail Deborah treated her problem with the utmost professionalism. Deborah carried out the treatment for my daughter, in our home, so as to ensure my daughter was as comfortable as possible. My daughter was extremely nervous and Deborah immediately put her at ease by explaining exactly what she was doing and why. A very painless and successful moment later the problem was gone and my daughter was a very happy little girl again. Whilst treating this Deborah also gave me advice on how to avoid this in the future, including what instruments were best and how to use them.

01

Care at Tidy Feet

Nail Surgery

A procedure where either a portion or the whole toenail is permanently removed — often because of damage, involution or an ingrown toenail, and sometimes infection. At Tidy Feet, surgery and aftercare are offered together.

Partial or full nail removal · Local anaesthetic · Surgery and aftercare · Package pricing from the clinic fee list

Care at Tidy Feet

Insoles

Insole therapy is provided where appropriate, following an in-depth consultation and assessment. Custom devices are made locally by an orthotist in Northamptonshire; over-the-counter devices are supplied by Sidas.

Consultation before prescription · Custom casted devices · Footbed-type insoles · Recover of existing devices

03

Care at Tidy Feet

Gait Analysis

Gait analysis observes and helps explain patterns of movement. It often supports understanding of injury or disease processes, links closely to assessment before insole prescription, and in some cases leads to a referral to a surgeon.

Movement pattern assessment · Injury and disease context · Linked to insole planning · Referral when appropriate

Call 01926 945 007

Verruca Treatment

Verruca treatment options include needling and K-Laser. Verruca needling is recognised as a treatment that does not leave scar tissue; anaesthetic makes the procedure comfortable. Treatment forms part of a package that includes follow-up appointments.

Needling with anaesthetic · K-Laser options · Package including follow-up · Discussed before treatment starts

Call 01926 945 007

05

Care at Tidy Feet

General Footcare & Referrals

Foot care depends on each patient’s needs. Care is reviewed as short-term issues resolve or longer-term health changes. Tidy Feet refers patients to other services, consults with GPs, and liaises with podiatry and orthopaedic surgeons when needed.

Individual care plans · Ongoing review · GP and specialist liaison · New-patient and follow-up appointments

Laser Treatment

K-Laser · Lunula Laser · Fungal nails and verrucae · Soft-tissue support · Package explained before treatment

About

Tidy Feet Podiatry

Tidy Feet Podiatry was opened in Southam in September 2007 by Deborah Coleman. It has steadily grown, and since 2010 has included a clinic in Stratford-upon-Avon. Deborah lives locally and either walks or rides her bike to work.

Straight answers from tidyfeet.co.uk — booking, clinics, hours and who you’ll see.

01How do I book an appointment?+

Call 01926 945 007 or 07807 635 112. The website’s “Book a consultation” link goes to the contact page; there is no separate online booking system published.

02Where are the clinics?+

03When are you open?+

Southam is open Tuesday and Wednesday 8:00–12:30 and 13:30–18:00, with Friday by special appointment. Stratford is open Thursday 8:30–17:00, with Monday by special appointment.

04Who will I see?+

Deborah Coleman, podiatrist. BSc (Hons) MChS, member of the Royal College of Podiatrists, HCPC registration CH8559. Qualified since 2007; previously a nurse (RGN).

Appointments usually last 30 minutes. Assessment appointments (pre-surgery, diabetic, neurological, gait) and the initial appointment last about 45 minutes.
