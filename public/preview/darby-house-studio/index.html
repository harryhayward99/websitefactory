Why Darby House

Independent advice. Proper aftercare.

Our business has years of experience with multiple manufacturers and we are not tied to any one supplier. This means we can offer truly independent advice to our clients. We do not employ pushy sales people and our team are invested in your project through to the very end. The big retail companies cannot match our after-care service.

Lee who started the business has 20+ years experience working for some of the biggest manufacturers and distributors in the fitted furniture industry. This knowledge is vital to providing a high quality product to our clients at a fair price. You’ll be surprised at the quality (and price) we offer.

We can offer a full project management service to just supply only. We can do as little or as much as you like. Ask for full details of all of our services — and let us transfer your dream into reality.

Our process

From first measure to enjoy.

We do as little or as much as you want — design only, supply only, or full project management.

01

Free design visit

We measure, collect ideas, then leave you to your evening. Full-colour renders follow.

02

Quote & choices

Agreed design, then a clear price — including finishes, electrics and trades if needed.

03

Order & install

Enjoy
