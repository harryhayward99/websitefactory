How it works

Process withoutthe hard sell.

Email your plans or we come to you. We measure, design and quote — then you choose how much of the project you want us to run.

01

Free initial appointment & design

Email your plans, or we can come to you. Our appointments don’t take up all of your time. We will not try and sell you a kitchen, bedroom or bathroom right away on this initial visit.

The first visit is about measuring your room and collecting your ideas. Once this is done (it can take as little as 30 minutes) we leave you to your evening. We will contact you when the plan of your new room is ready and arrange to get a copy to you to discuss the layout. Our full colour renders put you front and centre in your new room.

02

Quotation & product choice

Once the design is agreed upon, we can begin to price the whole project for you. We can quote for wall and floor coverings, lighting, plastering, electrical and gas work.

Do not be afraid at this stage to say if you would like a supply only price. Equally if you have a friend that is a tradesman, we can work with them to achieve the finished result. We do as little or much as you want.

03

Ordering & installation

If our quote is accepted, we get to work right away by booking a site survey with our tradesmen. This gives you peace of mind and also allows you to meet the professionals that will be carrying out work in your home.

Once a deposit is paid we can arrange a fitting date and a fully detailed schedule of works will be provided to allow you to live around us while we make your ideas come to life.

04

Enjoy

Enjoy your new room, safe in the knowledge that we are always here for you.
