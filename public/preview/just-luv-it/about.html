About us

Luxury gifts with real meaning

We love pieces for your home that bring real joy — handmade over mass-produced, sustainable over plastic, supporting individuals and communities in the UK and abroad.

Meet Andrea

I’m Andrea, and I just love beautiful gifts and homeware. I love to give presents that have a story behind them, and to furnish my home with one-of-a-kind pieces that are good to the planet as well.

I’ve worked as a senior manager within a large organisation for years, but like many people, lockdown changed my perspective and Just Luv It came about.

What about the name? I’m a Brummie, and I call everyone “luv” — it’s in my blood. When I started looking for stock, “I just luv it” was on my lips every time I found a fab new product.

Another very important part of Just Luv It is my husband, Howard — without him, we wouldn’t be here doing what we do. He’s by my side at every market and every pop-up.

Fairness

The pieces we choose are never mass produced, and each one can be traced back to the person who made it — a person who gets a fair deal for their work.

Sustainability

Our products must do no harm to the planet, and can be effectively recycled as appropriate. We reuse and recycle wherever possible.

Quality

We are proud of every piece we sell, and would love to receive each one as a luxury gift ourselves.

Our promise

When you buy from us, we promise an ethically sourced, handmade, sustainable product bought from its producer at a fair price. Fair to suppliers, fair to the planet, and a beautiful product for you.

  • V Viable, sustainable materials
