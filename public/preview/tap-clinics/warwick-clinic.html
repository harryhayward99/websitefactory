Warwick

4 The Holloway, Warwick, CV34 4SJ

The original clinic, open since 2015, a short walk from the Market Hall. Face the Hair & Beauty Gallery and the door is up the small staircase on the right, opposite the Rose & Crown. There is no waiting room, so arrive at your appointment time and take the chair just inside the door. Easiest parking is Sainsbury's, with two hours free, then a short uphill cut-through. If stairs are difficult, the Stratford clinic is step-free.

Hours. Open seven days, 8am to 9pm, by appointment.
