Your visit

Warwick has on-street parking nearby and two hours free at Sainsbury's, about five minutes' walk, uphill at the end. There is no waiting room. Stratford-upon-Avon has parking directly outside, a waiting area, and step-free access. Leamington Spa is beside St Peter's multi-storey.

For a first appointment, wear something you can move in, such as shorts. New clients complete a consent form. If you are late, the session may be shorter and the full fee still applies. Please give one business day, 24 hours, if you need to cancel or move. After that the full fee applies, unless the slot is filled. A text to 07904 839521 is enough. Payment is due at the end unless you have prepaid, by cash or card.

Private insurance

TAP is not registered directly with health insurers. Many policies still cover physiotherapy with any HCPC-registered physiotherapist. You pay for the session, and the clinic gives you an invoice with your physiotherapist's HCPC number so you can claim it back.

Check the policy first, including any excess, GP referral or pre-authorisation. Shortfalls and declined claims are not covered by TAP. Email info@therapyandperformanceclinic.co.uk before you book if you want to check.

FAQs

Where are you?

Do I have to play sport?

No. The team also sees office workers, parents, people after surgery, and weekend walkers.

Physiotherapy or sports massage?

Physiotherapy is for a problem that needs assessing and rehab. Sports massage is soft-tissue work for tightness and recovery. If you are unsure, book a physio-led assessment.

What happens first?

A history, a look at how you move, and hands-on treatment in that visit. You should leave knowing what is going on, not only with a sheet of exercises.

What can you help with?

Back pain, neck pain, knee pain, hip pain, sciatica, shoulder problems, plantar fasciitis, sports injuries and rehab after surgery are the problems the clinic lists. Ask if yours is not on that list.

How often should I have a massage?

Some people come once. Others book regularly while they are training. The team says they will recommend what you need rather than sign you up for more.

Can treatments be combined?

Yes. A session might include needling on one area and hands-on work elsewhere.
