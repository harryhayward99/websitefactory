Questions, answered.

Do I need a GP referral?

No. Book online as a private client.

How soon can I be seen?

Usually the same week. Warwick and Stratford-upon-Avon are open seven days, 8am to 9pm. Leamington Spa currently runs on Fridays, with occasional Wednesdays.

How much is it?

The clinic's own short answer is £65 for a 60-minute appointment. The booking menu is more specific: many 60-minute massages are £65, a physiotherapy initial is £70, and a Mummy MOT or Full Body MOT is £100. See the menu.

Do you take insurance?

TAP is not registered directly with insurers. Many policies still cover physiotherapy with an HCPC-registered physiotherapist, and you can claim back with the invoice. Read the detail.
