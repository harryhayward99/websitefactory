About

Southam Chiropractic

Chiropractic is concerned with the diagnosis, treatment and prevention of mechanical disorders of the musculoskeletal system, and the effects of those disorders on the nervous system and general health. This practice has a specialist interest in neck and back pain, and takes physical, emotional and social wellbeing into account.

The chiropractors are also trained to work with pregnant women, babies and children, including gentle care during pregnancy and treatment suitable for children and babies.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

From the clinic’s own pages: the first visit, the report of findings, who they see, and when they are open.

01What happens at the first visit?+

The clinic asks what you have come for, which may be symptom relief, improving function, preventative care, maintaining health or a check-up. The initial consultation includes a case history and an examination of the spine, joints, nerves and muscles. It takes 30–40 minutes.

02When do I hear the findings?+

After the initial consultation the chiropractor prepares a report of findings and discusses the course of action and treatment plan, if one is needed. Under normal circumstances that conversation is on a different day, so there is time to think and to bring questions.

03Which conditions do you see?+

The clinic lists joint pain, headaches and migraines, muscle pain and spasm, sciatica, back pain, neck pain, shoulder pain and pelvic pain. It has a specialist interest in neck and back pain.

04Do you see children and during pregnancy?+

The clinic says its chiropractors are trained to work with pregnant women, babies and children, including gentle care for discomfort during pregnancy and treatment suitable for children and babies.

05Is there parking?+

There is a free car park with ample spaces behind the clinic and often spaces outside the front as well.

06When are you open?+

Monday, Tuesday and Thursday 10:00–20:00. Friday 10:00–16:00. Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday closed.
